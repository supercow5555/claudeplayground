The problem

Every student books at a different time — some 6 months out, some 3 weeks out. A single fixed deadline breaks for late bookers. A single departure-relative rule breaks for early bookers. We needed a model that works correctly for any booking window.

Three constants — calculated once at booking
SymbolNameMeaning
tBooking dayCalendar day the student booked
dDeparture dayCalendar day the student departs
λ = d − tBooking windowTotal days between booking and departure. Fixed at booking, never changes.
Why we don't use t, d, and x directly

t, d, and x are calendar dates. They are different for every student and hard to reason about universally. Instead we use two relative variables that mean the same thing for every student, regardless of when they booked or when they depart.

Three variables — updated daily
SymbolNameMeaningFormula
xTodayToday's date—
ϕDays since bookingHow long the student has had the appϕ = x − t
γDays until departureHow close the student is to leavingγ = d − x
λ = ϕ + γ

Always true, for every student, at every point in time. Days since booking plus days until departure always equals the total booking window.

Two types of rules
ϕ-based — booking-relative  ·  triggers when ϕ ≥ threshold Use when urgency depends on time since booking. Example: passport and parent contact need to be collected fast after booking, regardless of departure date.
γ-based — departure-relative  ·  triggers when γ ≤ threshold Use when urgency depends on how close departure is. Example: health info and emergency contact matter as departure approaches.
Grace period

No task becomes overdue until ϕ ≥ grace period (default 3). No student ever opens the app on day one to find tasks already overdue — everyone gets a minimum window to act before anything turns red.
